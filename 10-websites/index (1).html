About Electroduction

Electroduction started with a simple rule: build real, working software instead of tutorials. Every project here runs entirely in the browser with zero build steps, from a full shopping cart to a live chat interface, each one deployed and usable the moment you open it.

It's a working record of design and engineering decisions made and shipped, one project at a time.
