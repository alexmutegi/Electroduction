AIRSOFT & PAINTBALL TWO ACRES
The Field, Split By Day

Two sports, one plot of dirt.

Fridays & Sundays

Airsoft Scenario Nights

Team-based objective games across the full two acres — CQB structures, a wooded flank, and a mid-field bunker line. Full-send scenarios run every other Friday under lights.

  • Rental AEG package: $35, includes gun, mask, 2000 BBs
  • Bring-your-own rig: $18 field fee
  • Chrono check mandatory at check-in, 400 FPS cap
Saturdays

Paintball Speedball

Inflatable bunker field for fast, close-range games — points play in rotating brackets through the afternoon, open sign-up at the gate.

  • Rental package: $40, includes marker, mask, 200 paintballs
  • Bring-your-own marker: $20 field fee
  • Paint sold at the gate only — no outside paint on our field

Before You Play

Field rules, no exceptions.

  • 01Full-seal eye protection on at all times past the gate line — no exceptions, no grace period.
  • 02Chrono check on arrival for any bring-your-own airsoft replica; anything over 400 FPS sits out.
  • 03Blind-fire and physical contact are both game-ending — one call from a marshal and you're off the field.
  • 04Minimum engagement distance of 15 feet on full auto, enforced on both fields.
Good to Know

Getting ready to come out.

Closed-toe shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dirty — long sleeves recommended for airsoft. Water's free at the gate, but the café next door is open through the last game slot if you need a real meal after.
